Privacy Policy – Reuma journal

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Reuma journal is a personal health journaling app designed to help users keep track of symptoms, pain, stiffness, medication, exercise, nutrition ideas, goals, and personal notes.

1. Data stored in the app

Reuma journal may store information that you enter into the app, such as:

  • pain and stiffness information;
  • information about painful joints;
  • daily goals and personal notes;
  • medication information entered by you;
  • exercise information and preferences;
  • nutrition-related information entered by you.

This information is stored locally on your device. The developer of Reuma journal does not receive or have access to this information.

2. No collection of personal health data

Reuma journal does not collect, transmit, sell, or share the health and journal information you enter into the app with the developer or third parties.

Your information remains on your device and is not sent to a remote server by Reuma journal.

3. Accounts

Reuma journal does not require you to create an account or provide personal information in order to use the app.

4. Backups

The app provides functionality to create and restore a backup of your journal data. Any backup you choose to create is under your control. If you save or share a backup outside the app, that copy may be stored in the location or service you choose.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete the information stored in the app using the deletion function available within Reuma journal. You can also remove the app from your device.

If you have created or saved a backup outside the app, you are responsible for deleting that backup from the location where you saved it.

6. Third-party services

Reuma journal does not use third-party advertising networks, analytics services, or other third-party services to collect your personal health information through the app.

7. Children's privacy

Reuma journal is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

8. Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app or its privacy practices change. The latest version will always be made available through this webpage.

10. Medical disclaimer

Reuma journal is intended as a personal journaling and tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnoses or personalized medical treatment. Information and suggestions within the app are not a substitute for advice from a doctor or other qualified healthcare professional.
