Reuma journal – Support

Welcome to the support page for Reuma journal.

Reuma journal is a personal journaling app designed to help users keep track of symptoms, pain, stiffness, medication, exercise, nutrition ideas, goals, and personal notes.

Need help?

If you have questions, experience a problem with the app, would like to provide feedback, or have suggestions for future improvements, please contact us by email.

Email: infiteclips@gmail.com

Privacy

For information about how Reuma journal handles your information, please see our Privacy Policy.

Data and backups

Reuma journal stores your journal information locally on your device. The developer does not receive or have access to the health and journal information you enter into the app.

If you create a backup, you control where that backup is saved or shared.

Medical information

Reuma journal is a personal journaling and tracking tool. It does not provide medical diagnoses or personalized medical treatment and is not a replacement for advice from a doctor or other qualified healthcare professional.
